History

From Streetly to Royal Sutton Coldfield

The Royal Sutton Coldfield Philatelic Society began as the Streetly Philatelic Society, which appears to have been founded in 1969. The exact founding date and first meeting place have yet to be confirmed, but a special postmark issued in 1994 records the society’s 25th anniversary.

The Early Stamp Fairs

The society held its first stamp fair and exhibition in Sutton Coldfield on 20 March 1971. A special postmark marked the occasion. By 1983, Royal Mail’s British Philatelic Bulletin was advertising a society fair at Streetly Community Centre in Foley Road East. Later fairs there brought together dealers in stamps, postal history and postcards.

The Silver Jubilee

On 7 May 1994, the society celebrated its silver jubilee by hosting the Midland Philatelic Federation’s Spring Convention at Sutton Coldfield Town Hall. The event featured competitions and 30 dealers’ stands. It was also the occasion of the first meeting of the newly formed Association of British Philatelic Societies. A special Sutton Coldfield handstamp marked the anniversary, and a separate souvenir cachet showed Concorde over Sutton Coldfield.

Collecting Sutton Coldfield’s Postal History

Among the society’s presidents were the air mail specialist and stamp dealer Francis J. Field and his son John Charles Walsh Field. John served as president for many years and helped build the society’s collection of Sutton Coldfield postal history. At a morning meeting shortly before his death in 2013, he presented another item for the collection.

Streetly Collection

A New Name

For a time, the society held evening meetings at Streetly Methodist Church Hall in Blackwood Road and monthly morning meetings at Sutton Coldfield Methodist Church in South Parade. In September 2016, it became the Royal Sutton Coldfield Philatelic Society, a name chosen to better reflect where its members came from. The society continues to meet at Sutton Coldfield Methodist Church.
